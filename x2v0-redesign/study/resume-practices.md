# Resume design practice (research, 30 Oct 2026)

Why the second design looks the way it does. Most sources are career blogs and job-board guides rather than studies, so read these as industry consensus, not evidence.

## What the sources agree on

- **Single column for the document that gets submitted.** Two-column and table layouts can scramble when an applicant tracking system (ATS) reads left to right. ([Jobscan](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/resume-tables-columns-ats/), [techinterview.org](https://www.techinterview.org/post/3233474603/ats-friendly-resume-formatting-engineers/))
- **No skill bars, charts or timeline graphics.** Parsers keep the words and drop the graphic, so a timeline or a rating bar adds nothing a recruiter's software can read; career timelines should be plain dated job entries. ([dev.to: skill bars](https://dev.to/cvpilot/why-your-cvs-skill-bars-are-invisible-to-the-parser-and-what-actually-gets-indexed-13gi), [Hiration](https://www.hiration.com/blog/skill-bars-resume/), [cvowl](https://cvowl.com/post/can-ats-read-resume-charts-and-graphs))
- **Hierarchy through size and weight, not boxes.** Name → section → details; two typefaces at most; body 10–12 pt; generous white space. ([Made Good Designs](https://madegooddesigns.com/resume-design-guide/), [Standard Resume](https://learn.standardresume.co/design/visual-design), [Yoh](https://www.yoh.com/blog/8-expert-formatting-tips-for-designing-a-resume-that-lands-interviews))
- **Academic roles read a CV, not a one-page resume.** Publications belong inside it; committees score against the job's person specification. ([IEEE Computer Society](https://www.computer.org/publications/tech-news/career-watch/cv-template), [Lancaster RSE person specification](https://hr-jobs.lancs.ac.uk/Upload/vacancies/files/12549/RSE%20DASS%20Person%20Specification%202025%20(1).pdf))
- **Research Software Engineer postings ask for** experience in an academic research environment and an understanding of research software sustainability. ([Johns Hopkins RSE posting](https://riseme.ai/jobs/johns-hopkins-university/d63fe1e1-147d-43ac-bc5a-70b90eea5848/research-software-engineer-data-science-and-ai-institute), [Leeds RSE JD](https://jobs.leeds.ac.uk/Upload/vacancies/files/15042/CSUIT1269%20G7%20Research%20Software%20Engineer%20JD%20FINAL.pdf))

The "6–7 seconds per resume" figure that circulates had no study behind it in what I found, so it is not used here.

## How the second design applies it

- The career timeline bar is gone; dates sit next to each job.
- On screen: a sticky profile column (name, summary, section nav, contacts) beside the record. On paper and phones: one plain column, as the ATS advice asks.
- One typeface family: Fira Sans, a humanist sans (Erik Spiekermann's design, in the FF Meta line) with full Cyrillic. Hierarchy from weight and size only.
- Publications stay inside the page, for academic readers.
